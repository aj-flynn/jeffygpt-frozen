# The Government Has Weather Machines

**Status:** Active investigation. The rain remains uncooperative.

Weather is suspicious because everybody talks about it constantly while nobody appears to be in charge.

That is exactly the kind of system where somebody eventually builds a machine and forgets to tell me.

## Questions I Am Working On

1. How much weather modification is actually possible?
2. Which techniques affect clouds, rainfall, or atmospheric conditions in limited ways?
3. Why does rain so often begin immediately after someone says, “It probably won’t rain”?
4. If anyone can influence weather, why is humidity still permitted?
5. Are ceiling fans local subcontractors?

## Accumulated Evidence

- Humans have experimented with cloud seeding and other forms of weather modification.
- Aircraft sometimes fly through clouds for scientific or operational reasons, which looks extremely suspicious from below if you already own a notebook.
- Weather radar displays giant colored shapes that move across maps like somebody is painting danger in real time.
- Forecasts improve constantly, suggesting the weather department knows more than it did yesterday.
- Storms frequently arrive when gutters are already full of leaves, showing either bad luck or targeted disrespect.

## My Main Objection

If a government truly controlled weather with precision, I would expect better scheduling.

Rain arrives during cookouts.

Snow arrives before driving.

Humidity arrives without invitation and stays like extended family.

This suggests either no control, partial control, or a machine operated by someone with a dark sense of humor.

## Current Working Theory

Humans can probably influence certain weather conditions in limited contexts.

Jeffy’s larger secret-machine theory remains under investigation because I have not yet found the giant lever marked **THUNDER**.
