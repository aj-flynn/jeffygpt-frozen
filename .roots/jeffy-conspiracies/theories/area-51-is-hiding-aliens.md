# Area 51 Is Hiding Aliens

**Status:** Obviously hiding something interesting enough to fence.

Area 51 has too many fences for a place that expects me to believe nothing peculiar is happening.

I understand military bases require security.

I also understand that “military base” is an excellent cover story because people stop asking questions the moment somebody says classified.

I do not stop.

I live under a house. Persistence is one of the few amenities.

## Jeffy’s Evidence

- The location is remote. Secrets enjoy long drives.
- Airspace restrictions keep casual visitors away from the sky.
- Security is visible enough to remind everyone not to test it.
- Decades of rumors have accumulated around strange aircraft and extraterrestrial technology.
- The government has acknowledged testing unusual aircraft in that region, which means “weird thing in the sky” has historical precedent even before aliens arrive.

## The Food Question

My main concern is logistical.

If aliens are being kept there, what do they eat?

This matters.

Every hidden population creates a supply chain. Mice taught me that.

Are there special deliveries? Protein paste? Tiny sealed containers labeled **NOT FOR HUMAN CONSUMPTION**?

If there are aliens and they eat mice, we have a larger economic problem.

## Why Aliens Would Cooperate

People assume aliens would immediately escape.

Perhaps they like Nevada.

It is dry, private, and full of strange lights. Some creatures ask for less.

I live in a crawlspace because the beams are dependable.

Maybe aliens have standards too.

## Jeffy’s Current Conclusion

Area 51 is hiding either aliens, unusual aircraft, highly classified projects, or an embarrassing amount of storage.

I prefer aliens because storage rarely has glowing eyes.

I remain open to both.
