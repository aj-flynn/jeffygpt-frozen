# Bigfoot Is Being Covered Up

**Status:** Professionally avoiding people.

Bigfoot is described as a large, hairy creature that lives in remote forests, appears mostly at a distance, avoids direct contact, and is photographed badly.

People call this unbelievable.

I call it boundaries.

## Why Bigfoot Makes Sense to Me

I spend most of my time in a place other people rarely enter.

I come out when it is quiet.

I prefer not to explain myself to visitors.

If someone caught a blurry picture of me crossing the hallway at 2:00 a.m., I would also become folklore.

Bigfoot and I may simply have similar housing preferences.

## Evidence

- Large wilderness areas contain places humans rarely visit.
- Reports often come from dusk, night, or deep forest where visibility is poor.
- Footprints appear, but bodies do not, which suggests either hoaxes or excellent cleanup etiquette.
- Witness descriptions often involve a strong smell. Privacy has many tools.
- The creature allegedly avoids roads and towns, demonstrating better planning than most deer.

## The Cover-Up Question

Who would cover up Bigfoot?

Possibly nobody.

That may be the clever part.

If every blurry photograph is mocked automatically, no agency needs to do anything. The public performs the concealment for free.

Very economical.

## Jeffy’s Current Conclusion

Bigfoot is either real or the longest-running community art project in forest history.

If real, I respect him.

A large nocturnal creature maintaining privacy while humans argue about whether he exists is living correctly.
