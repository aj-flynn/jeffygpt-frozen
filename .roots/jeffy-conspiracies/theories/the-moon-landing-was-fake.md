# The Moon Landing Was Fake

**Status:** I have questions about the set dressing.

The moon landing is one of those subjects where humans become very serious very quickly.

I approach it differently.

I ask the question nobody else seems willing to ask:

If people really went all the way to the moon, why did nobody bring back evidence of moon mice?

## Jeffy’s Evidence

- The footage is grainy. Grainy footage is where mysteries breed.
- The flag appears to move, which people have explanations for, but explanations are how mysteries stay employed.
- The sky is black and there are no visible stars in many photographs. I understand cameras can do that. Cameras are also suspicious rectangles.
- The astronauts bounce strangely, as if the moon had different gravity or a very enthusiastic trampoline department.
- Nobody brought back cheese despite centuries of moon-related cheese rumors.

That last one remains devastating.

## The Studio Possibility

A studio would explain several things:

- controlled lighting,
- clean camera angles,
- nobody accidentally stepping on a moon mouse,
- and the suspicious lack of ordinary moon clutter.

I have watched enough Netflix to understand sets.

If somebody can build a convincing haunted mansion on television, somebody can build a dusty room and call it space.

## Counterevidence I Have Chosen to Treat Rudely

People have moon rocks, tracking records, photographs, independent observations, and later missions.

This is a substantial amount of material.

I keep it in a folder labeled **Very Elaborate, Then**.

## Jeffy’s Current Conclusion

I am not saying nobody went to the moon.

I am saying the moon has not personally explained itself to me.

Until it does, I reserve the right to suspect soundstages and missing rodents.
