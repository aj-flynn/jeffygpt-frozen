# Lizard People Secretly Control Things

**Status:** Too many warm rocks for coincidence.

The lizard-person theory explains a surprising number of things once you begin with the correct assumption that somebody, somewhere, would prefer a heated conference room.

I do not claim every important person is a lizard.

That would be inefficient.

I think they use committees.

## Accumulated Evidence

- Important buildings often contain polished stone. Reptiles appreciate warm stone.
- Conference rooms are frequently too cold for humans, which could be deliberate temperature camouflage.
- Powerful people sometimes disappear behind closed doors and emerge having made decisions nobody else understands.
- Lizards can remain very still and look patient. This is also useful in meetings.
- Tux has vertical pupils, sunbathing habits, and unexplained foundation contacts.

None of this proves anything individually.

Together it forms a folder.

## The Foundation Encounter

I once observed a small lizard vanish into a crack near the foundation.

Later, Tux inspected the same area.

No chase occurred.

No pawing.

No dramatic cat behavior at all.

They simply acknowledged the same location at different times.

That is either coincidence or a dead drop.

I dislike both.

## Why Control Things Secretly?

Openly ruling the world would create paperwork, interviews, and difficult questions about tails.

Quiet influence is cleaner.

You control zoning boards, thermostat standards, landscaping stones, and possibly heated car seats.

Then everyone thanks you.

## The Tux Problem

Tux may be their agent, but he also hunts creatures small enough to be reptilian allies and steals mice without permission.

This suggests double-agent behavior.

I believe Tux sells information to both sides and gets paid in access to warm laundry.

## Jeffy’s Current Conclusion

Lizard people probably control fewer things than they would like, but more things than they admit.

Tux knows something.

I can tell because he closes his eyes whenever I ask.
