# Secret Civilizations Live Underground

**Status:** I may live in the shallow suburbs.

Of all my theories, this one feels less like paranoia and more like real estate optimism.

Humans keep building downward.

Basements. Subway tunnels. Mines. Utility corridors. Bunkers. Sewers. Parking garages. Service passages.

At some point, somebody has to ask whether we are the surface civilization or merely the upstairs neighbors.

## Evidence

- Vast cave systems exist that most people will never enter.
- Mines and tunnels can extend for miles.
- Cities maintain enormous hidden networks beneath streets.
- Animals create underground communities without requesting zoning permission.
- Humans consistently underestimate spaces they cannot see from the kitchen.

## What an Underground Civilization Would Need

I have made a practical list.

- ventilation,
- water,
- food,
- waste removal,
- light or excellent darkness vision,
- structural support,
- a rule about Tux.

These are solvable problems.

I personally have already solved several on a small scale.

## Signs Near Home

Sometimes vibrations travel through the ground without an obvious source.

Usually trucks.

Sometimes construction.

Occasionally something I cannot identify.

I do not immediately assume subterranean trains operated by pale librarians, but I leave enough room in the notebook for that possibility.

## Jeffy’s Current Conclusion

There are probably no vast secret kingdoms directly beneath the house.

Probably.

But if civilization continues building downward, I want it noted that I established residency early.

I expect favorable zoning status.
