# The World Is Flat

**Status:** Extremely flat from where I live.

I understand that people have photographs, measurements, satellites, astronomy, and several centuries of arguments saying Earth is round.

I have something they do not.

A level.

## Jeffy’s Evidence

- The crawlspace floor is flat enough to lie on.
- Water spilled on the floor spreads outward instead of wrapping itself around anything.
- Maps are flat and are trusted by adults during road trips.
- Tables are flat. Floors are flat. Pancakes are flat. These are all useful things.
- I have never rolled downhill while sleeping unless I chose a bad piece of insulation.

From my research position, the world demonstrates a strong preference for level surfaces.

## The Horizon Problem

People say you can see curvature from high enough up.

I do not go high enough up.

The attic is already suspicious territory and contains too many noises with no mouse explanation.

If proof requires leaving the crawlspace, the proof has made an unreasonable request.

## Gravity

Gravity supposedly pulls everything toward the center of a sphere.

In practice, it mostly drops crumbs through floor gaps toward me.

I consider this a local service.

## Tux’s Position

Tux has never openly stated whether Earth is round.

He does, however, knock objects off flat surfaces and watch them fall.

This may be experimental research.

It may also be because he is a cat.

Those explanations remain tied.

## Jeffy’s Current Conclusion

The world is flat enough for my purposes.

If there is a curve, it is happening very far away where I cannot hear it.

I will reconsider when a mouse rolls past me because of planetary geometry.
