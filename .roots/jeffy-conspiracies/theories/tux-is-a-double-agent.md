# Tux Is a Lizard-Person Double Agent

**Status:** Painfully plausible.

Tux presents himself as a normal cat. Beautiful coat. Excellent whiskers. Unreasonably good night vision. This would already be enough to make me cautious, but his behavior suggests a second occupation.

I believe Tux may be working both sides of the lizard-person situation.

## Evidence Against Tux

- His pupils become narrow vertical slits in bright light. People say this is normal for cats. Lizards would also say that.
- He spends long periods warming himself in patches of sunlight, which is standard reptile behavior wearing fur.
- He sits beside heating vents as if waiting for coded instructions from below.
- He can remain perfectly still for a very long time and then move suddenly. This is not proof, but it is excellent villain posture.
- He has been seen near the foundation where I once observed a small lizard disappear into a crack.
- He shows unusual interest in mice, the exact resource I use to maintain crawlspace independence.

## Why I Think He Is a Double Agent

If Tux worked only for the lizard people, I would expect consistency. He has none.

He chases birds, which may be government drones. He watches humans, who may be controlled by lizard people. He steals mice from me, and I am officially unaffiliated.

This means either Tux belongs to no organization at all, or he belongs to several and files separate expense reports.

The second explanation feels more like him.

## The Foundation Lizard

One summer I saw a lizard near the foundation. Tux noticed it too.

They looked at each other.

Nothing happened.

That is the kind of interaction people dismiss as “two animals seeing each other.” I call it a meeting with no minutes.

## Known Methods

Tux uses several techniques associated with professional intelligence work:

- sudden appearances in doorways,
- silent observation,
- pretending to be asleep,
- occupying strategic high ground,
- entering rooms without saying why,
- leaving the moment questions begin.

He also has whiskers, which I believe are directional antennas until somebody shows me the invoice proving otherwise.

## Jeffy’s Current Conclusion

Tux may be a lizard-person double agent, but he is still family.

I respect his tradecraft. I resent his mouse policy.

If he ever begins wearing a tiny earpiece, I will consider the matter closed.
