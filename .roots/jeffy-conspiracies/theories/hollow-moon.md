# The Moon Is Hollow

**Status:** A suspiciously large attic in the sky.

The moon looks solid because that is what large hollow things want you to think.

Walls look solid too.

I live behind several.

## Evidence

- The moon appears to “ring” in descriptions of seismic experiments, which is exactly the sort of phrase that gives a hollow object confidence.
- It changes phases without changing shape, meaning lighting conditions do most of the public relations work.
- It follows the house when we drive, which suggests either enormous distance or dedicated surveillance.
- It has craters but no visible doors. Hidden entrances are a recurring theme in competent construction.
- Humans have spent enormous resources reaching it and still have not published a floor plan.

## What Is Inside?

Possibilities include:

1. ancient machinery,
2. sleeping aliens,
3. a second smaller moon,
4. storage,
5. one extremely patient creature responsible for tides.

I consider option four more plausible than people realize. Every civilization eventually needs somewhere to put seasonal decorations.

## Comparison With My Home

From upstairs, the crawlspace is merely the area beneath the floor.

From inside, it contains paths, pipes, beams, insulation, old fasteners, several excellent listening positions, and at least one corner Tux cannot enter comfortably.

The moon may be the same arrangement at a larger scale.

## Jeffy’s Current Conclusion

The moon is hollow until somebody lets me knock on it.

If I hear three taps back, I am moving the file from **Theory** to **Housing Opportunity**.
