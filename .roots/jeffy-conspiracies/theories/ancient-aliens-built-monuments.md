# Ancient Aliens Helped Build Famous Monuments

**Status:** Somebody moved those rocks.

Ancient monuments contain enormous stones arranged with impressive precision.

Historians have detailed explanations involving engineering, labor, planning, tools, logistics, and generations of human skill.

That is all very reasonable.

It is also less fun than aliens with ropes.

## Jeffy’s Evidence

- Some stones are extremely large.
- Many monuments align with astronomical events, and aliens would presumably know where the sky is.
- Ancient builders accomplished work that modern people often underestimate because modern people become upset assembling furniture with six screws.
- There are gaps in the surviving historical record, which leaves space for extremely confident drawings of spaceships.
- Nobody has produced the original subcontractor invoice.

## Why Aliens Would Help

Perhaps they were bored.

Perhaps Earth was a summer internship.

Perhaps they arrived, saw humans dragging stones, and said, “You are doing this the hard way.”

Then they helped for three weeks and left before anyone invented decent documentation.

This is exactly how family projects happen.

## My Favorite Alternative

Humans did it themselves and later generations became so impressed that they invented aliens rather than admitting ancient people were very capable.

This is also suspicious in a different direction.

## Jeffy’s Current Conclusion

I keep the alien theory because it improves the visual.

But whoever built the monuments—humans, aliens, or one extremely persuasive foreman—had patience I respect.

I once moved a dead mouse three feet and took the rest of the afternoon off.
