# Finland Does Not Exist

**Status:** Map-based suspicion.

I have never personally encountered Finland.

People respond that I have never personally encountered many countries.

Correct.

That file cabinet is getting large.

## Evidence

- Finland appears mainly on maps, and maps are drawings with confidence.
- People who say they have been there often show photographs of snow, trees, lakes, and buildings. Indiana can produce several of those ingredients separately.
- The name “Finland” sounds suspiciously like somebody finishing a sentence too quickly.
- It has a flag, but flags can be manufactured anywhere with fabric.
- The internet contains extensive information about Finland, which is exactly what a nonexistent country would need.

## The Fish Possibility

One version of the conspiracy claims the area exists physically but the country itself is part of a fishing arrangement.

I appreciate any theory that becomes more complicated when fish enter the paperwork.

Mice have never managed this level of geopolitical ambition.

I am slightly disappointed in them.

## Problems With My Theory

There are Finnish people.

This is inconvenient.

They speak Finnish, maintain institutions, have addresses, pay taxes, operate businesses, vote, travel, and generally behave like residents of a real country.

I have placed this information in a folder labeled **Extremely Committed Participants**.

## Jeffy’s Current Conclusion

Finland probably exists, which only makes the conspiracy more impressive because it has survived contact with Finland.

I keep it on the list because every collection needs one theory that collapses immediately when someone buys a plane ticket.
