# Jeffy’s Favorite Conspiracy Theories

## Theories

| Conspiracy theory | Jeffy’s evidence summary |
|---|---|
| [Birds Are Government Drones](theories/birds-are-government-drones.md) | They sit on power lines, watch yards all day, and somehow nobody asks who trained them. |
| [Tux Is a Lizard-Person Double Agent](theories/tux-is-a-double-agent.md) | He may be feeding household intelligence to the reptiles while independently stealing everybody’s mice. |
| [Ceiling Fans Control Local Weather](theories/ceiling-fans-control-local-weather.md) | They spin indoors and shortly afterward wind exists outdoors. Timing matters. |
| [The Thermostat Is Listening](theories/the-thermostat-is-listening.md) | People complain about the temperature and then it changes. Very responsive for a supposedly dumb rectangle. |
| [The Refrigerator Light Interrogates Leftovers](theories/the-refrigerator-light-interrogates-leftovers.md) | The light only comes on when the door opens, which is exactly when witnesses are present. |

| [Wi-Fi Routers Feed on Secrets](theories/wi-fi-routers-feed-on-secrets.md) | People whisper near phones and the little router lights become extremely busy. |
| [The World Is Flat](theories/the-world-is-flat.md) | Floors, maps, and water all keep behaving suspiciously level from Jeffy’s research position. |
| [Lizard People Secretly Control Things](theories/lizard-people-secretly-control-things.md) | Cold meeting rooms, warm stone buildings, one suspicious foundation lizard, and Tux’s questionable loyalties have created questions. |
| [The Moon Landing Was Fake](theories/the-moon-landing-was-fake.md) | The footage is strange, the moon is far away, and Jeffy has concerns about the total absence of lunar mice. |
| [Area 51 Is Hiding Aliens](theories/area-51-is-hiding-aliens.md) | Too many fences, too much desert, and nobody will answer Jeffy’s most important question: what are the aliens eating? |
| [The Moon Is Hollow](theories/hollow-moon.md) | It rings, changes lighting, follows the house, and has no visible windows. Excellent concealment. |
| [Bigfoot Is Being Covered Up](theories/bigfoot-coverup.md) | A large nocturnal creature avoiding people and photographs sounds less impossible than professionally sensible. |
| [The Loch Ness Monster Is Real](theories/loch-ness-monster.md) | Deep dark water is just a crawlspace with worse breathing and stronger privacy. |
| [Finland Does Not Exist](theories/finland-does-not-exist.md) | Jeffy has never personally encountered Finland, while maps have already admitted they use symbols. |
| [Secret Civilizations Live Underground](theories/underground-civilizations.md) | Humans keep building downward, and Jeffy suspects he may simply live in the shallow suburbs. |
| [Ancient Aliens Helped Build Famous Monuments](theories/ancient-aliens-built-monuments.md) | The stones are huge, the stars are involved, and nobody has produced the original subcontractor invoices. |
