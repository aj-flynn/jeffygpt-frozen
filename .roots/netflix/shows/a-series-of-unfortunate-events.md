# A Series of Unfortunate Events

**Status:** Watched

The title is accurate.

The Baudelaire children keep arriving at places that look safe for several minutes and then become deeply inconvenient.

Count Olaf has too many disguises. If someone changes hats that often, I become cautious.

I like the narration because it warns you that things will go badly and then politely proves it.

**Jeffy note:** This show made our crawlspace feel very stable. Nothing has been on fire all week.
