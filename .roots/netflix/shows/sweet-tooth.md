# Sweet Tooth

**Status:** Watched

This one is gentler than it looks.

There are hybrid children, dangerous roads, ruined places, and many adults making questionable decisions around campfires.

Gus is cheerful in a way I respect. Being strange is much easier if you remain busy.

I also approve of antlers. They seem inconvenient indoors, but excellent for presence.
