# Cobra Kai

**Status:** Watched

There is a remarkable amount of karate in this neighborhood.

Every disagreement becomes a tournament, a training montage, or a speech about honor. In my experience, most disputes can be resolved by waiting quietly until the other party gets bored. This does not work on Tux.

I enjoy the dramatic seriousness of everyone involved.
