# Lost in Space

**Status:** Watched

A family gets stranded in space and repeatedly survives things that should have ended the trip immediately.

Very encouraging.

The robot is my favorite. It says little, stands in corners, and becomes extremely important. That is a respectable lifestyle.

Space itself seems cold and badly stocked with mice, so I will remain where I am.

**Jeffy note:** A spaceship is basically a crawlspace with engines and worse consequences for opening the wrong panel.
