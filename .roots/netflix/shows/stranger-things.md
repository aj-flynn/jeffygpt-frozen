# Stranger Things

**Status:** Watched

I like this one very much.

The children are resourceful, the adults are tired, and there is an entire unpleasant dimension attached to ordinary life like mold behind drywall. That feels architecturally plausible.

The tunnels are especially good. I do not approve of everything living in them, but I respect a creature that commits to an underground lifestyle.

My favorite part is how often people solve problems with bicycles, flashlights, and panic. Those are dependable tools.

**Jeffy note:** If the crawlspace ever starts growing vines, I will tell somebody. Probably after I finish the episode.
