# Alice in Borderland

**Status:** Plan to watch

People are trapped in a strange version of Tokyo and have to survive dangerous games.

I generally prefer games where losing means somebody else washes a dish.

Still, I respect mysterious rules. Tux and I have been playing a game for years where the rules change every time he wins.

**Reason it is on the list:** I want to study whether reading the instructions carefully helps. Nobody does that enough.
