# Locke & Key

**Status:** Plan to watch

A family moves into an old house full of magical keys.

This seems like an unreasonable number of features to disclose after closing.

I am interested in the keys because ordinary keys are already powerful. One small piece of metal decides whether a room belongs to you or becomes a mystery.

Magic only makes the locksmith situation worse.

**Reason it is on the list:** I want to see how long it takes everyone to stop putting dangerous keys in convenient drawers.
